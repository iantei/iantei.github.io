About

Add a headshot here — images/headshot.jpg, ~190px wide.

I am a software engineer in New York with 6+ years building systems that have to be fast and correct at the same time — a 5 ms real-time processing library under an FDA-regulated release, lock-free concurrency with explicit memory ordering, and a 1 TB pipeline cut from 10 hours to 30 minutes.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Arizona State University (4.0) and a B.Tech. from MNNIT Prayagraj. I currently build an AI-powered application on AWS against the Anthropic Claude API at Exito Tech. Before that I was a research software engineer at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, interned on Yahoo's edge infrastructure team fuzzing production DNS in C++17, and spent four years in Philips Ultrasound R&D.

What I actually enjoy is the layer where correctness gets hard: race conditions in update propagation, memory behaviour under a deadline, and the consistency guarantees a replicated store is really providing. I have implemented Read-Your-Writes and Monotonic Writes with Lamport clocks from first principles, and I have spent enough time in a 20 GB legacy C++ codebase to have opinions about how that work should be done.

I contribute to open source — four merged PRs to routee-compass, the Rust routing engine behind Google Maps' eco-routing, recognized with the U.S. DOE–VTO AMR Team Award 2025.
